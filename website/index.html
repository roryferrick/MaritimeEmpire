Maritime Empire

Build a shipping empire on a living globe.

Buy your first little Scooter, find a trade that pays and grow into a fleet of hundreds, sailing between 324 real ports through the canals, the ice and the storms.

A whole world of shipping

Trade what the world makes

Twenty commodities, from coffee and toys to iron ore, cattle and cars, and sixteen kinds of seafood, each cheap where it's really grown, built or caught and expensive where it's needed. Your ships buy low at one port and sell high at the next, and every trade feeds your company's level.

How trading works

Every port is a working harbour

Zoom in from the whole globe to a single pier. Every port has its own marina with berths, tugs, moorings and a town round it, and ships come in on their own side of the channel, tie up and pull out again.

Explore the world

Build your fleet

Seven lines of ships, from coastal container ships to supertankers, giant car carriers and reefers, plus fishing boats, crabbers, tugs, icebreakers and recovery boats. Each one has its own captain and crew, burns fuel, wears out and needs a good route to earn its keep.

Meet the ships

Canals, ice and weather

Queue for the locks at Panama and Suez, push through the Arctic with an icebreaker, ride the ocean currents and ride out the storms. Seasons close the Great Lakes in winter and bring snow to the land.

Sea and sky

A living sea

Whales, dolphins, sharks, seals and otters swim the oceans, and bird colonies feed round the harbours and follow your ships out to sea, each with its own call.

Wildlife and birds

Rivals and friends

Compete with AI shipping companies, or found companies with friends over Steam or your local network. You can even call down storms on your rivals with Divine Actions.
