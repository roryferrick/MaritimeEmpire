Shipyards, orders and the second-hand market

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • Ships of tier 2 and up (Feeder size; not tugs) are ordered, not bought: the Shop's button reads "Order…" and the name popup lists the yards that build the model, cheapest first. A deposit (20%) is paid at once and the rest on delivery. A cheap yard sometimes runs a month late. The ship is delivered at the yard's port, its parts at full from a fine yard and a little under from a cheap one, with a card and a line in the log; if the money for the rest isn't there the ship waits at the yard. Tier-1 boats and tugs are bought outright at the HQ or a hub as before.
  • The second-hand market: 12 ships the paper firms are selling, a third replaced each month, each a tier-2+ model from the firm's fleet with an age (3-20 years), hours at sea, a condition per part (0.35-0.95 less a little for age) and a price of 40-80% of list by condition, less for age; lying at the firm's home port, where it's delivered (the firm gets the money). Buying names it and needs the company type as usual; the ship comes with its parts as they are, so the major ones want a drydock.
