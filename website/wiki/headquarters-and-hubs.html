Headquarters and hubs

  • The company starts with its headquarters (HQ) at its home port. It can found any number of hubs: the first after the HQ costs $5M and each next twice the last, from any port's popup ("Build a hub here ($5,000,000)", click again to confirm; greyed with the reason when short), and each is permanent. Each hub (not the HQ) pays a monthly upkeep by its port's size, with the crew's wages.
  • Hubs have no upgrades for now: facilities to buy (berths, cranes, a fuel depot, a repair yard, offices) are planned. A hub launches ships, takes no port fee on its company's trade there, and has plots for warehouses.
  • Hub warehouses (2026-10-14): a hub that isn't the HQ has its company's warehouses on the HQ's half of the quay behind its basin, roofed in the company's colour so whose hub it is shows from above, 2 at first and one more for every 300 deliveries unloaded there up to 4, the one nearest the basin its freezer (a white roof with a band of the colour and cooling units), each with its shadow, ridge, roof sheeting and loading doors. The HQ building, the warehouses and the industry are now drawn after the port's town (its ground reached over the quay and hid them all).
  • On the map, the HQ and hubs have a ring in the company color around their port (heavier for the HQ; gray on the Route Assignment map).
  • New ships launch at the HQ or any hub, chosen when buying; recovery boats are then re-based across the hubs (see Lost ships and recovery boats).
  • Warehouses: every HQ and hub has room for 3, on the quay behind the marina's basin, on the side across the channel from the HQ building (+y), in a row of equal square plots nearest the basin, all open. Each is one commodity's, and serves every good of its kind (a maker's cars count as Cars, diesel as Crude oil): a warehouse, or a stockyard for livestock, a cold store for seafood and fresh produce, a terminal for oil and gas; a hub can't have two of the same, but the same one can be built at other hubs. Each costs a flat $15M and can be pulled down (confirmed) for half back, freeing the plot. At its port, for its owner's ships only, its commodity costs 5% less to buy and sells for 5% more. Built from the Hubs page (each card's Warehouses section) or by clicking a plot on the map (a popup with the same section): an open plot shows "Build ($15M)", which opens a picker of every commodity (ones already there greyed; not the makers' goods, which use their kind's); a built one shows its boosts and "Pull down". On the map an open, empty plot of this player's own hub is marked out with a dashed line; built ones are a grey warehouse with a band in the good's colour, at any company's hub. In multiplayer they're host commands. Saved with each hub.
