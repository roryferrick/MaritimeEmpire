2. Ships screen

  • Tabs over the list like the Shop's (2026-10-15): All, then a tab per company type (Cargo & Ore, Vehicles, Agriculture, Tankers, Seafood) and Service (recovery boats, tugs, icebreakers), each with how many of its ships the Show filter lets through ("Tankers (17)"); a company type's tab is left out while the company has no ships in it and doesn't have the type. The list opens on All.
  • Scrolling list grouped by model in the Shop's order (each type's lines, then the service ships, each line smallest first) under a heading per ship line in its color ("Container ships", "Ore carriers"...; hidden with nothing under it), each group on a faint tint of its ship line's color (the same colors as the Prices menu and Finances) under a picture of the model (bow to the right) and its name in that color, 8 compact tiles wide, one tile per owned ship. Groups with no ships (or none matching the filter) are hidden.
  • Each tile has its captain's face (a small round picture) beside three lines: the ship name (cut short with "..." if it doesn't fit), then its cargo in smaller text ("Coffee"; recovery boats say "Recovery"). A ship that needs the player says why instead, in red ("Held in port", "Paused", "No route", "Lost at sea", "Waiting for toll money"). Then "Capt. " and its captain's name. Hovering a tile shows the full name and its status line (e.g. "Docked at Korsakov — not enough fuel for Hong Kong; turn on refuel").
  • Each tile shows the ship name, a status dot, and thin maintenance / fuel / cargo bars. Green = running (has route, not paused, not held); amber = waiting in port for money for a full load; red = docked (paused at a port, no route, or held in port).
  • Clicking a tile opens the ship popup.
  • Sort (within each model, left to right) by earnings, name, status (lost, then at risk, then held, then stopped, then running) or profit all time; show all ships, running, stopped, lost, or recovery boats. A count shows how many are shown. Tiles re-sort when the fleet or the sort changes, not live.
