Finances screen

  • Tabs at the top (they wrap onto a second row on a narrow screen): Company (the whole company: all below), one for each of the company's types (2026-10-15: Cargo & Ore, Vehicles, Agriculture, Tankers, Seafood, Cruises, Research, as it has them), and Compare companies. A type's page shows only that type: its totals are its ships' own money (sales, cargo bought, fuel, repairs, tolls and port fees, wages, from their ledgers all time and their share of the last 10 minutes) and their operating profit, then the type's own money (Cargo & Ore industry, Vehicles factories, Agriculture farms, Tankers rigs and refineries, Cruises supplies, Research contracts and gear) and the type's total; its Delivered card only its goods (the plain Cars, Trucks and Boats of older saves under Vehicles); its ship table only its lines ("Profit by ship: Vehicles"). The bank, loan, logo and canal rows are the Company page's only. Compare companies: a table of every company in the game, ranked and numbered by operating profit all time, the most first (that column marked ▼; this player's marked "(you)", names in their colors): company type (icon and name; see Company types), company value, money, ships, hubs, operating profit over the last 10 minutes and all time, and units delivered.
  • On the Your company page, at the top, "Keep in the bank" (Nothing, $100,000, $250,000, $500,000, $1M, $2.5M, $5M, $10M, $25M, $50M or $100M; saved with the company, default Nothing): ships won't spend that money on cargo, so it builds up for buying ships or upgrades. Fuel and repairs can still use it, so no ship is stranded. A ship waiting for a full load says so ("... (keeping $250,000 in the bank)").
  • Totals for the last 10 minutes and all time: cargo sales, cargo bought, fuel, repairs, canal tolls, port fees, crew wages, loan interest, ships bought, ships sold, hubs founded, hub upkeep, divisions opened, ship refits, logo changes, cruise supplies, farm land bought and sold, farm costs, farm store sales, and the rest of the businesses' lines, and operating profit (sales − cargo bought − fuel − repairs − tolls). Smaller text and tighter columns so it all fits.
  • Beside the totals, a "Delivered" card: everything the fleet has delivered, all time, a total for each unit (Containers, Tons, Head of livestock, Vehicles) with each commodity under it when a unit has several (Toys, Clothing...; Oil, Gas, Iron ore...).
  • Then a card per canal the fleet has used: "Panama Canal: 12 crossings · tolls −$48,000".
  • A table of every ship: model, sales, cargo bought, fuel, repairs, tolls, lifetime profit and profit over the last 10 minutes, grouped by ship line in shop order (container ships, ore, grain, livestock, tankers, vehicles, recovery boats). Each group sits on a faint band of its line's color and starts with a total row: the line's name in its color, its number of ships, and each column summed, in slightly bigger text. Click a column to sort the ships within each group; click a ship's name to open its ship popup. Refreshes every second.
  • The table's money is shortened to 3 figures from $100k up ("$23.1B", "-$571M", "$985k") so it fits the screen however big the numbers get; hover a figure for the exact amount. The totals card shows exact amounts.
  • The totals and the 10-minute window are saved with the game.
