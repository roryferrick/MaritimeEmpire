Technology

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • The tree: 30 techs in six branches (engines, hulls, propulsion, cargo gear, navigation and electrics, green systems) of three tiers, each either a perk (a factor on fuel, speed, wear, breakdowns or port time for the whole fleet, some only for categories: LNG-ready designs, wind-assist rotors) or an upgrade for a part above the stock one (common-rail retrofit, air lubrication, contra-rotating propellers, vacuum mooring, biofuel blending kit...). Perks apply the moment a tech is held; upgrades are fitted in drydock ship by ship, and ships ordered from a yard come with every held upgrade fitted.
  • The R&D companies: 20 (engine makers, classification societies, propeller works, cargo systems, bridge electronics, green shipping) at real ports, each publishing its branch's techs in their years (2016 to 2028, a date in the year from the seed). A published tech is licensed for a fee: the tier's share of the company's fleet at list price (0.4/0.8/1.5%), at least the tier's floor ($250k/$500k/$1M); a company an R&D company likes pays 30% less and is offered the tech a year early. Publication and early offers come as cards (License it / Research page / Later).
  • Own R&D: one tech at a time, progressed each month by a budget ($20,000 a point, times the CTO's Engineering factor and 1.5x with a Technology centre at a hub: a new hub facility, $30M) and a quarter of the banked research points from the research ships; at the tier's cost (400/1,000/2,500 points) the tech is held. Finished before the world publishes it, it is held exclusively for two years: royalties a month by tier ($40k/$90k/$200k) and the world's publication waits. Selling survey data: 100 research points to an R&D company for $600k and liking.
