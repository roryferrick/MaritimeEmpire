Running the firms you control

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • A paper firm the company holds half or more of (Ownership.CONTROL) gets a card on the Ownership page with its figures (ships on the books and the hulls they stand for, ships at sea, last month's sailings, cash, last month's earnings, worth) and its plan: a focus (world-wide, or voyages out of its home country only), the trade groups it takes (its lines, ticked), and the dividend (the share of each month's earnings paid to the company; the rest builds the firm's cash).
  • Orders: each month a run firm with no order out and the cash for three ships asks, with a card, to order the model it has most of from the cheapest yard with a slot; approved, it pays in full from its own cash and the ship joins its books on delivery. The card's "Order a ship now" button asks at once. The ships added are remembered and put back after a load, since the firms are made again from the seed; a save now also keeps the capacity the fleets were dealt from, so they come back the same.
