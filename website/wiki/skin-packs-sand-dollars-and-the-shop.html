Skin packs, Sand Dollars and the Shop

  • Skin packs are bought for Sand Dollars, the in-game currency: one Sand Dollar is one cent, and they're bought in bundles (500 for $5.00 up to 5,000 for $50.00) with Steam money through the Steam Inventory Service. Without Steam running the Sand Dollars page says so and nothing can be bought. The balance, the packs owned and the pack worn are this computer's, not a save's, with a ledger of every grant and purchase.
  • The Shop: two tabs. Skin packs lists every pack on sale (and any owned), with what's in it and its price, a Buy button (greyed with how much more is needed), and for an owned pack Wear on my ships / Take off: one pack's ship skins are worn at a time, on the player's own ships only (other players' and the rivals' ships are drawn as they are). Sand Dollars shows the balance, the bundles and what Steam says about a purchase under way.
  • A pack: ship skins by model; CEO and captain outfits; crew hats and uniform colours.
