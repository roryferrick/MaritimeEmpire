Skin packs, Sand Dollars and the Shop

  • Skins are bought for Sand Dollars, the in-game currency, one at a time or in bundles: any one skin 100 Sand Dollars ($1), every ship skin of a pack 500 ($5), every outfit of a pack 500 ($5), a whole pack 1,000 ($10); a bundle never charges for skins already owned (its price is capped at the missing skins one by one). A ship skin covers the models its art fits (the drumstick and the dinner roll come in three fits, one skin); an outfit is a human skin: the CEO and captain options it unlocks, its crew hat and its uniform colours (the catalog's outfits list what each holds; a shared option such as buckle shoes comes with any outfit that has it). Sand Dollars: one Sand Dollar is one cent, and they're bought in bundles (500 for $5.00 up to 5,000 for $50.00) with Steam money through the Steam Inventory Service. Without Steam running the Sand Dollars page says so and nothing can be bought. The balance, the packs owned and the pack worn are this computer's, not a save's, with a ledger of every grant and purchase.
  • The Shop is opened from the title screen, its button under Single Player and Multiplayer, not from the Esc menu. It has two tabs. Skin packs lists every pack on sale (and any with a skin owned), then the packs coming soon, shown with every skin and its picture but "Coming soon" in place of every Buy. For each pack: its title, how many of its skins are owned (what the ships wear is chosen in the Inventory, not here, and buying a skin doesn't put it on them), a Whole pack button, then its ship skins and its outfits, each skin its own panel, four to a row: its picture (a ship skin's art; an outfit worn by a whole-body portrait), its name, which models wear it or what the outfit unlocks and Buy with its price (Owned once bought) and an All of these button per group, every bundle button showing its price now and greyed with how much more is needed. Sand Dollars shows the balance, the bundles and what Steam says about a purchase under way.
  • The Inventory is opened from the title screen, its button under the Shop and above Quit: every skin the player owns, in two tabs. Ship skins: the pack worn, then By ship, each model with a skin owned for it, then every owned ship skin by pack with its picture, who wears it and "Wear on its ships" (sets it for each of its models; "✓ Worn" once it is). A model's choice wins over the pack worn, and a ship's own Skin (its popup, now "As the Inventory" for the default) wins over both. Outfits: every owned outfit with its whole-body portrait and what it unlocks, and where it's chosen (Found Company and the CEO editor; crew hats and colours on the Uniforms page).
  • A pack: ship skins by model; CEO and captain outfits; crew hats and uniform colours.
