Ownership

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • More shares when it issues or lists. The price a share is the company's value over the shares, times the market's sentiment once listed; a monthly history drawn on the page.
  • Each outside holder is a character with an opinion of the CEO that a rising price and dividends warm and losses and a falling price cool. The founder votes their own shares, the public votes with sentiment, the rest by opinion and traits. A divided vote comes as a card; when outside holders set against the CEO (opinion -40 or worse) hold more than half the shares, a no-confidence card removes the CEO and a successor is chosen as for a death.
  • Quarters: the quarter's profit is income less costs (capital moves excluded); the dividend is paid out of a positive quarter to the outside holders (the founder's part stays). A listed company reports against expectations (the last quarter's profit plus 5%, or 2% of value at first): sentiment moves by the miss or beat, clamped 0.3x-3x, with a card and a headline.
  • Past CONTROL (50%) the firm sails in the company's colour and the company takes its share of the firm's earnings each month. Running a controlled firm comes with M4.
  • The IPO: the board votes, then a bank is chosen (BANKS: Harbour & Keel to Goldwater Sachs; fee 4-7% of what is raised against reach, each taking companies over a minimum value), which values the company; the price range round it and the share to sell (10-49%, new shares) are set. Listing day a week later on the home region's exchange (EXCHANGES), the price settling in the range by demand with a pop or a sink, the money in less the fee, a card and a headline. Everything is host-run in multiplayer.
