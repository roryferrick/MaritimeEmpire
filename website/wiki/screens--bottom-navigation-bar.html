Bottom navigation bar

  • World | Ships | Finances | Shop. Markets, Hubs, Farms and Makers are pages over the map (see 1. World (map) screen). Hidden on the Route Assignment screen. Esc opens the Esc menu from every tab.
  • The Ships tab shows a black dot (every alert dot on a button is black with a thin pale ring, to show on the red buttons) while any ship needs the player: lost with no recovery boat on the way, held in port, waiting at a canal for toll money, docked and paused, a docked cargo ship with no route, or upgrade points to spend. Its tooltip counts each reason and names the ships ("1 held in port (Tundra Godwit)", up to 3 names then "and 2 more"). The Ships screen filter "Needs attention" shows just those ships.
  • The Shop tab shows a black dot while any ship can be bought (of the company's types, and affordable); its tooltip lists them.
