People

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • Everyone is a character: the CEO, the six executives, the department heads and the ships' officers. A captain's Seamanship on the ship's speed and fuel, Engineering on its wear and breakdowns, Leadership on its crew's wages. Hooked where those are figured.
  • All Net commands. Salaries are paid with the crews' wages each period.
  • Department heads: a head takes offers of their trade group (a ship category) whose load port is in their market region, under their remaining budget (the value of the contracts they hold), when an idle ship of the company's (docked, no route or paused, no contract) carries the good and can reach the load port and make the leg; they sign at the rate nudged by their Negotiation, assign and route the ship, and the log says so. When no ship is idle, a head may propose taking a routed ship off its route at its next port for a contract that would pay more a day than that ship's route has made over the last 30 days: a card pauses the game (Take the contract / Keep it on its route), at most one a week; approved, the ship's route is kept and it goes back to it when the contract ends. A ship's Keep on route toggle exempts it. Contract premiums were brought from 1.3-2.0x to 1.1-1.65x of plain freight (about 2.5x spot income a capacity-day in play-tests).
  • Ageing: everyone ages. An executive or head leaving empties the post; an officer is replaced; the CEO's going brings the succession card; the new CEO's traits and skills are the company's, the story notes the handover, and the headshot button follows.
  • Family: a new company's CEO over 30 is usually married already; after that the CEO may marry and have children. The heir is the child named on the People page (or the eldest of age): on the CEO's death or retirement the succession card offers them first, and they take the founder's shares and the post (the player plays on as them); when an executive or a hire takes over instead the founder's shares stay with the family as "the X family". The CEO card and the People page's Family card show the family.
  • A breakdown costs 8, a mate promoted over the captain's head 10.
  • While sore, the firm is picked first among the bidders for tenders in its home country and bids up to half its soreness lower, and its blocks cost up to the soreness more; the Ownership page marks such firms.
  • Hiring by applications (2026-10-29, People): the job market is an inbox. Hiring from a posting closes it. Recruiters on retainer add half again to the flow, a skill point to applicants, and screen out the weakest unsolicited ones. The headhunter remains the instant, expensive way.
  • A raise (RAISE 15%, the Raise button on the People page) gives 15.
  • Someone glad in their post refuses half the time; otherwise a card pauses the game: match it (the salary; for a captain a double bonus and heart) or let them go (a captain's ship gets a new officer).
