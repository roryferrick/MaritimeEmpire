Company types

Added or changed on 15 October 2026

  • Every new company starts as one of six company types (a seventh, Research, can only be bought as a division: see Research), chosen first thing on the New Company screen: Cargo & Ore (container ships, ore carriers and breakbulk carriers, and its mines, plants and factories: see Cargo & Ore industry), Vehicles (car carriers and boat carriers, and the makers: see Vehicle makers and factories), Agriculture (grain, livestock and Fresh produce carriers, and farms: see Farms; the Shop tab once called Food), Tankers (tankers and gas carriers, and rigs and refineries: see Oil and gas), Seafood (fishing boats, crabbing boats and seafood carriers) and Cruises (Ocean and Expedition cruisers: see Cruise lines). Each line has five tiers (see Ship models).
  • All of its types' ships can be bought from the start (see Progression).
  • It can buy into one more type of the player's choice at any time, for good, for $5M: an Open a division button at the top right of the World map, an Open a division button on the Shop's locked tabs and a tutorial tip all point to it. The choice shows the types as cards, the types it has greyed; Choose, then Confirm.
  • Ships outside the types (recovery boats, tugs, icebreakers) are for every company.
  • Each type is shown as an icon with its name: beside each saved game on the title screen, in the Type column of Finances → Compare companies (every type the company has on hover), on the Shop's tab banners and the type cards.
  • Recommended headquarters follow the type: each port was ranked by how much its type's first ships could earn from it with only its home market region open (the best loop for cargo ships, the catch in season for fishing and crabbing boats, the best cruise for cruise ships), and ports a season closes are never better than hard starts. The New Company map and its notes and the join page follow the chosen type. The Great Lakes ports are offered to every type.
  • The tutorial follows the type: Mo B. Dick's welcome says what the company does, Sonny names its first ships, a Seafood start's first boat counts (it needs no route), the fishing tip only shows to a company with Seafood, and a "Time to branch out" tip comes once it can afford a division three times over.
