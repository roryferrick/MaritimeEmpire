Contracts

Added or changed on 22 October 2026

  • The producers offer the company shipping where the economy calls for it: a good their plants make at a port of a zone long of it (stock over 40 days of cover and the price under x0.95), bound for the main port of a zone short of it (price over x1.1), 200 nm or more away and one leg for a ship of the company that carries the good or, while it has no ship of the good's line, for the line's tier-one ship; offers that stop fitting are dropped at load and at each refresh. Kinds: a voyage (units = the company's biggest hold of the line, due in 3x the sailing days + 30) and recurring volume. The cargo is the producer's (loaded free out of the zone's stockpile, counted inbound like any cargo). The rest wait on the Contracts page (and the port popup names those loading there).
  • Standing: a producer's opinion is its country's standing plus its own memory; tiers Unknown <10, Known 10-40, Trusted 40-70, Renowned 70+. A new company is Known (20) at home. A delivery +0.5, a quarter met +3, a contract done +8, a quarter short -6 (and a fee of half the shortfall's pay), a voyage overdue -12 (and a fifth of its value), half to the country and half to the producer; everything fades 2% a month towards 0.
  • The game routes it load port to delivery port and back, so a ship on a contract is never left without a route. The offer card (2026-10-29) comes only for offers the company can sign (tenders always): Accept signs and opens the Contracts page at Your contracts; a signing that fails (the offer gone, standing short) is told in the activity log. At the load port, bound for the delivery port, it loads the contract's cargo first, spare hold for spot cargo; at the delivery port it's paid the contract's rate and the delivery is counted. A contract done or failed frees its ships. The company bids a rate per unit on the Contracts page; every tender announces itself with a card. At closing the producer takes the lowest bid weighted by its standing with the bidder: the company's win signs the contract at its bid (and a card says so), a loss to a named firm is news (and a card if the company bid). The department heads taking the small offers come with M3.
  • A short quarter brings a stern letter: carry the shortfall onto next quarter at no extra fee or pay the fee now.
  • Flavours (2026-10-22): a Seafood company gets supply deals; a Cruises company gets group charters. The Research contracts board is unchanged. Supply deals and charters need no ships assigned.
  • Tuned 2026-10-22 against spot runs: with the living world's flat zone prices the best spot run a day from a home port is small (a Feeder 100 about $1,000 a day from Rome, $2,300 from Rotterdam; a Mini Bulker about $100), while early offers pay $900-$1,500 a day for a Breakbulk Coaster on 250-400 nm legs and about $5,000 a day for a Scooter 20 carrying batteries, so contracts are the better living from the start. Offers go only to goods the company's divisions ship. One offer a lane at a time; a global producer is judged by the plant's country.
