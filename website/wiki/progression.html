Progression

Added or changed on 21 October 2026

  • The living world overhaul took out company levels and every other kind of XP (ship levels, hub levels, recovery boat and tug levels), market region unlocking, ship slots, ship skills and mega upgrades.
  • What a company may buy: every ship model of its company types (its divisions) and the service ships (recovery boats, tugs, icebreakers), as soon as it can pay. No slots: own as many as you can afford (tugs still come two per route port). Ships are just their model (no skills); ship components, refits and drydock are planned (M3 in the design).
  • Divisions: a company starts as one type and buys into any other at any time for $5M: the World map's Open a division button, the Shop's locked tabs, and a tutorial tip once it can afford three.
  • Hubs are bought: any number, the first after the HQ $5M and each next twice the last; each hub (not the HQ) pays an upkeep a month by its port's size band ($5k Small, $10k Medium, $20k Large, $40k Giant, paid with the crew's wages: "Hub upkeep" in the totals). Hub facilities to buy are planned.
  • Company value stands where the level was: in the top bar ("Worth $27.5M ▲", with an arrow when it's up or down over 1% on 5 minutes ago), in Compare companies, on the join page, in the Company value achievement line, for the Divine powers' prices, and for the HQ building's size on the map (one step each time it grows 2.5 times over from $2.5M, up to 10).
  • Storms, seasonal closures and breakdowns act from the start; ports only grow for now (shrinking comes back with the new economy). The calendar starts on 1 January 2016; its faster pace comes with the new economy.
  • Research expeditions earn research points, banked for developing the company's own technology (planned).
