Settings page sliders

Added or changed on 12 October 2026

  • The Settings page's General tab has three-step sliders in place of the radio buttons and switches: Ships seen from afar, Routes and Day and night. Birds and Tutorial stay switches. On the Audio tab the Master slider goes to 200%; the other volumes to 100%.
