Company logos

Added or changed on 15 October 2026

  • Every company has a logo: the mark of the type it started as on a shape (square, circle, triangle or diamond) in the company's colour and a second colour (any of the company colours but its own), laid out solid, bordered, split on the diagonal or with a stripe across; Swap colours trades the background's and the mark's.
  • Made on its own page when founding (New Company: types, the company, then "Your company logo", then the CEO), with a big preview and every choice drawn as it would look. Changing it later costs $1,000,000.
  • Shown beside each saved game on the title screen, beside each company's name in Compare companies, before the company's name in the top bar, on each card of the Hubs screen, as a small sign beside each company's HQ and hub rings on the map (and on its HQ building's roof or over its hub warehouses zoomed in), and on its cruise ships' funnels.
