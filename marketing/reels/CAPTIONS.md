## 1. Contracts and tenders (feature_01_contracts.mp4)

Producers don't wait for you. They put their shipping out to contract. ⚓

In Maritime Empire the work comes to you as offers: a single voyage, a quarter's volume, or a tender with sealed bids against named rivals. Your standing with each producer decides what they'll offer. Accept, assign a ship, and the cargo loads free and pays at the contract's rate. Miss a quarter and there's a fee.

Coming soon to Steam. Follow along for the voyage.

## 2. Shipyards and the second-hand market (feature_02_shipyards.mp4)

Ships aren't bought. They're ordered. ⚓

Pick one of 35 real yards, pay the deposit, and wait the months it takes to build her. A good yard builds well; a cheap one is cheap and cheerful. Or buy second-hand: her real age, her real parts, and she's yours where she lies.

## 3. Ship parts and drydock (feature_03_parts.mp4)

Every ship is built from real parts, and each one wears on its own. ⚓

Engine, hull, pumps, gear. Minor repairs happen at the pier; major ones only in drydock, at a shipyard port or a dock of your own. Upgrade a part and she sails faster, burns less and breaks less.
