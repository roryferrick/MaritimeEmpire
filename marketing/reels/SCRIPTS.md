# Maritime Empire — Reels scripts (draft for approval)

Format for all of them: 1080×1920 vertical, game footage only, pop-up text, no music bed baked in (you voice it, music can go on in Instagram). Every reel ends on the same 1.5 s card: **MARITIME EMPIRE · Coming soon to Steam**.

How to read each script:
- **VO** is what you say, split into beats with times. About 2.5 words a second, so a 15 s reel holds about 35 words.
- **TEXT** is the pop-up caption on screen for that beat (short, so it reads with the sound off).
- **SHOT** is the footage I'll capture from the game for that beat.

Mark each one ✅ / ✏️ (with your changes) / ❌. Times will shift to fit your actual read; I'll cut the footage to your recording.
